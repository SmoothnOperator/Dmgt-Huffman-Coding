Formal mathematical proof, loop invariants, and asymptotic graph bounds

Total: 𝒪(|Σ| log |Σ|) |V| = 2|Σ| − 1
Lemma 1 Greedy Choice
Sibling Lowest Frequencies

Let x and y be the two symbols in alphabet Σ with the lowest frequencies. There exists an optimal prefix code tree T* where x and y are siblings at maximum depth dmax.

WPL(T) = ∑ f(v) · d(v) → Minimized
Lemma 2 Induction
Optimal Substructure

Replacing lowest leaves x, y with a contracted meta-parent z of weight w(z) = w(x) + w(y) creates a subproblem Σ′ of size |Σ| − 1. An optimal tree for Σ′ directly yields an optimal tree for Σ.

WPL(T) = WPL(T′) + w(x) + w(y)
Heap Ops Min-Heap
Priority Queue Transitions

Each merge step performs:
• 2 × Extract-Min: 2 × 𝒪(log K)
• 1 × Min-Insert: 1 × 𝒪(log K)
Across all |Σ| − 1 iterations, the summation strictly bounds to 𝒪(|Σ| log |Σ|).

∑ 𝒪(log K) = 𝒪(log(|Σ|!)) = 𝒪(|Σ| log |Σ|)
Graph Invariant Full Binary Tree
Euler's Handshaking Theorem

Every merge couples two subtrees. The resulting graph is a strictly full binary tree (every internal node has out-degree 2).
• Leaves: L = |Σ|
• Internal Vertices: I = L − 1
• Total Nodes: |V| = 2L − 1
• Edges: |E| = 2L − 2

∑ deg(v) = 2|E| = 4L − 4
